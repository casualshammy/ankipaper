Review your Anki flashcards on a Kindle or e-ink browser

{{ brand_name }} is a free web app that talks to AnkiWeb and renders your cards in a server-rendered interface designed for slow monochrome browsers. Sign in with your existing AnkiWeb account — no separate signup, no install, no premium tier.

What you get

  • Works on Kindle and other e-ink devices. Built-in browser only — no app, no extension.
  • Uses your existing AnkiWeb account. Your decks, progress, and statistics sync back and forth with AnkiWeb, Anki Desktop, and AnkiMobile.
  • The real Anki scheduler. The same FSRS algorithm and Rust backend that Anki Desktop uses.
  • Free, no ads, no tracking. No analytics scripts, no premium tier, no upsells. Open source on GitHub.
  • Your data is yours. Decks and notes stay in your AnkiWeb account. {{ brand_name }} only caches what's needed to render cards in your browser.
